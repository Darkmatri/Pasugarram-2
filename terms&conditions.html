Understanding delivery slots, returns, quality guarantees, and subscription terms.

1. Freshness Guarantee & Inspection

All organic items are harvested prior to dispatch. If you notice any damage or quality variance upon doorstep delivery, you can request an instant 100% refund or free replacement within 24 hours.

2. Morning Harvest Delivery

Deliveries occur between 6:00 AM and 8:30 AM. Customers may choose doorstep delivery or unattended milk drops at designated apartment milk boxes.

Our Journey & Philosophy

Pasugarram was founded with a single mission: to make pure, chemical-free food accessible, affordable, and transparent for every family. Modern agriculture has depleted soils with synthetic fertilizers. We empower smallholder regenerative farmers across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and Kerala who restore soil fertility naturally.

From native heirloom tomatoes to wild forest honey from Nilgiri tribes, every product you receive directly supports fair livelihood for local farm families.

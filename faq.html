Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our organic certification, farm delivery cycles, and subscriptions.

How do you guarantee that vegetables and fruits are 100% pesticide-free?
We partner exclusively with NPOP and PGS-India certified farms. Soil and crop samples undergo regular lab testing for multi-residue pesticides. We also provide full farm traceability for every batch.
When is the delivery slot and how early does milk arrive?
Our primary morning harvest delivery occurs between 6:00 AM and 8:30 AM every morning. Milk is bottled the previous evening, chilled naturally, and delivered at your doorstep before your morning tea.
What is your cancellation and return policy?
If any vegetable, fruit, or dairy item does not meet your quality expectations, you can request an instant 100% refund or replacement within 24 hours directly from your profile dashboard with no questions asked.
How do subscriptions for A2 Cow Milk work?
You can set recurring daily or alternate-day milk subscriptions. You can pause or modify quantities anytime up until 9:00 PM the previous evening.

Need Immediate Assistance?

Our farm support team is available everyday from 6 AM to 9 PM.

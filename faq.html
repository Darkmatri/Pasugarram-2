Help & FAQs

Find answers to commonly asked questions about organic standards, delivery, and orders.

Are all Pasugaaram products certified organic?

Yes, all agricultural products are sourced from farms holding organic certification. We audit partner lands regularly to ensure zero chemical pesticides or artificial fertilizers are used.

How fast is the farm-to-table delivery?

We guarantee delivery within 24 to 48 hours from harvest. Fresh leafy vegetables and raw juices are cold-stored during transport to keep their original nutrients intact.

Do you support cashless online payments?

Currently, our platform supports Cash on Delivery (COD) and direct UPI integration upon delivery. Online gateway processing will be rolled out in our next phase update.

How can I track my placed orders?

Once you are logged in, navigate to your Profile page. You will see a detailed history of your past orders, along with their confirmation status and estimated delivery dates.

What is the shelf life of raw wild honey?

Pure unprocessed honey never spoils! Since we do not add preservatives or pasteurize it, natural crystallization may occur over time. Simply place the jar in warm water to reliquefy it.
